The Problem

Healthcare challenges facing Sierra Leone.

Geographic Barriers

Many rural communities are located far away from healthcare facilities.

Information Blackout

Limited access to verified healthcare information leads to misinformation.

Healthcare Worker Shortage

Sierra Leone has one of the lowest doctor-to-patient ratios in the world.
